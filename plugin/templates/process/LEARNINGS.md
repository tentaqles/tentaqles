# Learnings

Rules are promoted lessons: a lesson seen twice, or a change the human sent
back in review, becomes a rule here and (when it protects a property) a line
under "Must not trade off" in CLAUDE.md. The log is append-only.

## Rules
- R1 · <date> · <rule in one sentence> · from L<n>, L<m>

## Log
Format, one line each:
- `L<n> · <date> · <slug> · failure: <what> / root cause: <why> / fix: <what changed> / lesson: <one sentence>`
- `H<n> · <date> · <slug> · sent back · <what the human rejected and which check should have caught it>`
