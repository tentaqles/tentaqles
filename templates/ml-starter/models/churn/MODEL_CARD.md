## Intended use / Out of scope

- Intended use: rank active customers by churn risk once a month so the retention team
  picks whom to call first. A human decides every contact.
- Out of scope: pricing, credit, or any automated decision about a customer; customers
  in their first 7 days (too little tenure); any market other than the one trained on.
- Known misuse: reading the score as a calibrated probability per customer without the
  Brier check below, or using it to deny service.

## Limitations and risks

- Linear model: misses interactions between plan and tickets.
- Drift sensitivity: `tenure_days` and `support_tickets` carry most of the signal; a
  change in the support process moves the score.
- Feedback loop: customers who are called churn less, so later labels are biased by the
  model's own ranking. Keep a random control group out of the calls.

- Training command: `uv run python -m ml_starter.train`
- Environment: Python 3.11 or later, CPU only
