---
title: Pricing plans
document_type: reference
---
The Starter plan costs 19 dollars per month and includes 3 seats and 10 GB of storage. The Team plan costs 49 dollars per month with 10 seats, 100 GB of storage and priority email support.

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, single sign-on (SSO), audit logs and a dedicated account manager. All plans can be billed monthly or annually; annual billing saves two months.
