---
title: Configuring single sign-on
document_type: guide
---
Single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan. An administrator opens Settings, then Security, and uploads the SAML metadata XML from the identity provider. Okta, Azure AD (Entra ID) and Google Workspace are supported.

After saving, test the login in a private browser window before enforcing SSO for every member. Enforcing SSO signs out all active sessions.
